Behaviour Nodes: Engage, Investigate, Flee, Scan Area, Perch

Applies to LCM Nav3D 3.6.0 · Unreal Engine 5.2 to 5.8 · Updated 22 November 2026

LCM Nav3D ships ready-made behaviours for flying and swimming agents. Each one plans its own 3D route, moves the body through its Flight Movement component, and ends with Succeeded or Failed. That way you can build an agent's behaviour by putting nodes together instead of writing movement code.

Behaviour What the agent does Ends with
Engage Target Picks a firing position with a clear line of sight inside the weapon's range and gimbal limits, flies there, keeps the target in view as it moves, and sets Fire Allowed while a shot is good. Succeeded when its engagement window (Max Duration) ends. Failed when it loses the target and a short search at its last-known position does not find it, or no firing position can be reached.
Investigate Flies to a vantage point with a clear view of a last-known position, looks round, then searches on along the way the target was heading. Succeeded when it sees the target or finishes the search (Failed instead with Fail If Not Found).
Flee Gets away from a threat to cover: a point far from it, not toward it, that it cannot see into. Keeps re-checking as the threat moves. Succeeded once hidden and unseen for Hide Seconds.
Scan Area Sweeps a volume from a set of viewpoints, turning round at each, until the whole volume has been seen. Succeeded when the volume is covered, or early when the watched-for actor is seen.
Perch Lands on the nearest reachable surface tagged as a perch (rack tops, beams, ledges, pads). Succeeded once down; Failed when no perch is reachable.
Land / Take Off Touch down on a site (including a moving deck or a site under a roof) and climb away again, each archetype in its own way: drones descend vertically, birds and fish glide in with a flare. Succeeded once down / once at height.

Three editions of every node

  • Behavior Tree tasks, in the task list under their own names (Engage Target, Investigate, Flee, Scan Area, Perch, Land, Take Off).
  • StateTree tasks, under LCM Nav3D, for pawns driven by a StateTree component.
  • Mass crowd tasks, under LCM Nav3D (Engage (Group), Investigate (Crowd), Scan Area (Crowd), Flee, Perch), for Mass StateTrees.

The settings are the same in all three, so the reference below only lists them once.

Agents share the work

Agents on the same world never take the same perch, the same cover or the same firing position or viewpoint: each claim lives while its agent is heading for it or holding it. Pawns and Mass entities share one set of claims, so a flock of Mass birds and a pawn bird spread over the ledges together.

Wiring Engage and Investigate to AI Perception

  1. Give the pawn an AI Perception component with a sight sense, and make the target perceivable.
  2. Add the LCM Perceived Actor To Blackboard service to the branch. Set Result Key (Object) and, for Investigate, Last Known Location Key and Last Known Velocity Key (Vectors).
  3. Put Engage Target under a decorator on the Result Key with Observer Aborts: Lower Priority, and Investigate below it, reading the last-known keys. When the target is lost, Investigate searches where it went; when it is seen again, Engage takes over.

Perches and landing sites

Tag the surfaces agents may land on (an actor tag or a component tag, Perch by default) and set the Perch node's Perch Tag. For your own site selection, the EQS generator LCM Nav3D | Landing Sites and the test LCM Nav3D | Landable find flat, clear, reachable touchdown points for a flying agent; feed the best one to Land.

Demo levels to study

Every behaviour runs in the LCM Nav3D Samples demo levels (the free LCM Nav3D Samples download; see Installation): Hunter Drone (Engage), Harbour (Land on moving surfaces), Sea Cliffs (Perch, Flee, Take Off), Coral Reef (fish: Flee, Perch), Warehouse (Scan Area, Engage, Investigate, Flee, Perch) and Sentry Drones (perception, EQS firing positions, landing under cover), each in Behavior Tree, StateTree and Mass editions.

Reference

Engage Target

Settings (LcmBTTask_EngageTarget)

  • TargetKey - The actor to engage.
  • FireAllowedKey - Optional (Bool): true while a shot is good. Drive the weapon task's decorator with it.
  • AimPointKey - Optional (Vector): where to shoot - leads the target by the projectile's flight time.
  • Position - Where to take position (range and altitude bands, weights).
  • Route - How the route there is flown.
  • Tracking - How the target is tracked.
  • Sighting - Where sightings come from: the node's own line of sight, or the AI Perception sight sense.
  • TargetWalks - The target walks (a character): predictions stop at walls and follow the ground. Off for a flyer.
  • TargetRadiusCm - Body radius of the target, for the walker prediction.
  • MaxSightRangeCm - Farthest the agent can see the target from.
  • PositionLeadSeconds - Choose the firing position around where the target will be this far ahead.
  • FiringPositionQuery - Optional: an EQS query chooses the firing position instead of the built-in rings (querier = the pawn). Use the "LCM Nav3D | Engaged Target" / "Engaged Target (Predicted)" contexts to build it around the target. Asked every RechooseIntervalSeconds; when it finds nothing the built-in choice is used for that interval.
  • RechooseIntervalSeconds - Re-choose the firing position this often (seconds).
  • ProjectileSpeedCmS - Projectile speed for aim lead (cm/s). 0 = hitscan: aim at the target itself.
  • AimToleranceDegrees - The envelope: aim error, range band, and how long it must hold before firing is allowed.
  • GimbalMinPitchDegrees - The weapon sits on a GIMBAL that pitches independently of the body - a multirotor can only yaw its body, and a drone above a walking target must aim DOWN. The envelope therefore checks the body's YAW error against AimToleranceDegrees and the PITCH the shot needs against these limits (degrees, negative = down).
  • GimbalMaxPitchDegrees - The highest the weapon can aim (degrees above level; negative = below).
  • MinEngageRangeCm - Closest a firing position may be to the target (cm).
  • MaxEngageRangeCm - Farthest a firing position may be from the target (cm).
  • SettleSeconds - How long (seconds) the aim must hold inside the firing envelope before Fire Allowed turns on.
  • LostTrackSeconds - Not seen for this long: go to where it was last predicted to be.
  • MaxInvestigateSeconds - How long to look around the last-known position before giving up (Failed).
  • SearchTurnRateDegPerSec - Turn rate while looking around during Investigate (deg/s).
  • MaxRouteFailures - Consecutive route failures (no path to any chosen position) before the task fails.
  • DrawDebug - Draw the chosen position, the route, the aim line and the envelope state.
  • MaxDurationSeconds - Engagement window (seconds): the node succeeds when it has engaged this long. 0 = until aborted.
  • UseFlightMovementComponent - Drive the pawn through its LCM Flight Movement component when it has one; off moves the pawn directly.

Investigate

Settings (LcmBTTask_Investigate)

  • SearchLocationKey - Where to search: a Vector key, or an Object key (the actor's location when the search begins).
  • SearchVelocityKey - Optional (Vector): which way the target was heading - the search goes on that way.
  • TargetKey - Optional (Object): the actor being looked for - seeing it ends the search (Succeeded, FoundKey set).
  • FoundKey - Optional (Bool): true when the search ended because the target was seen.
  • ClearSearchKeyWhenDone - Clear SearchLocationKey when the search ends by itself (so the branch does not run it again).
  • Vantage - Where to look FROM: a point in this band round each search point with a clear view of it.
  • Route - How the route is flown.
  • ExtraSearchPoints - After the last-known position, search this many more points on along the way the target was heading (or round it, when the heading is unknown).
  • SearchSpacingCm - Spacing of those points (cm).
  • LookSeconds - Time spent looking round each search point (seconds).
  • SweepHalfAngleDegrees - Half-angle of the look-round sweep, either side of the search point (degrees).
  • Sighting - How the target is looked for (when there is one to look for).
  • MaxSightRangeCm - How far the agent can see the target (cm).
  • FailIfNotFound - Fail (instead of succeed) when the whole search ends without seeing the target.
  • DrawDebug - Draw what the node is doing in the level (Play-in-Editor debugging).
  • MaxDurationSeconds - Time limit (seconds): the search ends when it runs this long - Succeeded, or Failed with Fail If Not Found. 0 = until aborted.
  • UseFlightMovementComponent - Drive the pawn through its LCM Flight Movement component when it has one; off moves the pawn directly.

Flee

Settings (LcmBTTask_Flee)

  • ThreatKey - The actor to get away from.
  • SearchRadiusCm - Cover is looked for within this distance of the agent (cm).
  • MinDistanceCm - Distance from the threat the cover should be at (cm); nearer than MinDistanceCm is refused.
  • PreferredDistanceCm - Cover at about this distance from the threat is preferred (cm).
  • RequireCover - Cover must be out of the threat's sight (a collision line from its eyes). Off: exposure is only a cost.
  • RelativeHeightsCm - Heights tried round the agent's own (cm, relative): e.g. -300, 0, +300.
  • AzimuthSamples - Candidate density: azimuths per ring (rings every SearchRadiusCm / 4).
  • AgentRadiusCm - Radius of the fleeing body (cm), used to fit it into the cover.
  • CoverClearanceCm - Clear air round a cover beyond the body (cm): the route planner cannot end a route in a surface's shell, and a body that cannot hold still (a bird circles its cover) needs room for the circle.
  • DistanceWeight - Cost weights: staying far from the threat, a short run, not running TOWARD it, being seen.
  • TravelWeight - Cost weight: a short run to the cover.
  • TowardThreatWeight - Cost weight: not running toward the threat to get there.
  • ExposureWeight - Cost weight: a cover the threat can see into.
  • SwitchImprovement - A new cover must beat the kept one by this fraction.
  • RecheckSeconds - Re-check the chosen cover this often (seconds): the threat moves.
  • HideSeconds - Hidden and unseen this long: done (seconds).
  • Route - How the body follows its route: speeds, arrival and replanning.
  • DrawDebug - Draw what the node is doing in the level (Play-in-Editor debugging).
  • MaxDurationSeconds - Time limit (seconds): the flee ends (Succeeded) when it runs this long. 0 = until aborted.
  • UseFlightMovementComponent - Drive the pawn through its LCM Flight Movement component when it has one; off moves the pawn directly.

Scan Area

Settings (LcmBTTask_ScanArea)

  • AreaActorKey - Optional (Object): an actor whose bounds are the volume (a box, a volume, a room). Unset: the settings' AreaCenter / AreaExtent.
  • WatchForKey - Optional (Object): an actor to watch for - seeing it ends the scan (Succeeded, FoundKey set).
  • FoundKey - Optional (Bool): true when the scan ended because the watched-for actor was seen.
  • AreaActorTag - The area by actor tag, when no key is set (the first actor carrying it).
  • AreaCenter - The volume swept, when no area actor is given (world space).
  • AreaExtent - Half-size of the swept volume (cm), when no area actor is given.
  • ViewpointSpacingCm - Viewpoints are sampled through the volume's free space this far apart (cm).
  • MaxViewpoints - At most this many viewpoints (the farthest-apart are kept).
  • AgentRadiusCm - Radius of the scanning body (cm), used to keep its viewpoints clear of geometry.
  • ViewRangeCm - What the agent can see: range and horizontal field of view (degrees).
  • ViewFovDegrees - Horizontal field of view (degrees) of what the agent can see.
  • LookAroundDegrees - At each viewpoint: turn round this far (degrees) at this rate.
  • TurnRateDegPerSec - How fast the agent turns to look round each viewpoint (deg/s).
  • CoverageProbeSpacingCm - Coverage is measured on probe points this far apart through the volume (cm).
  • TargetCoverage - Done once this fraction has been seen (1 = fly the whole tour).
  • Loop - Fly the tour again and again (until aborted, a target is seen, or MaxDurationSeconds).
  • Sighting - How a watched-for actor is looked for.
  • Route - How the body follows its route: speeds, arrival and replanning.
  • DrawDebug - Draw what the node is doing in the level (Play-in-Editor debugging).
  • MaxDurationSeconds - Time limit (seconds): the scan ends (Succeeded) when it runs this long. 0 = until aborted.
  • UseFlightMovementComponent - Drive the pawn through its LCM Flight Movement component when it has one; off moves the pawn directly.

Perch

Settings (LcmBTTask_Perch)

  • PerchTag - Surfaces (actor or component tag) that are perches: rack tops, beams, ledges, rails.
  • SearchRadiusCm - Perches are looked for within this distance (cm).
  • FacePerchForward - Face the way the perch actor faces once down (a ledge looks out); off keeps the arrival heading.
  • AllowCoveredPerches - Perches under something (a shelf under the one above) are entered sideways.
  • KeepClearOfOthersCm - Stay away from perches another body is on or heading for within this distance (cm).
  • Profile - How the body lands: approach height, footprint, flare and touchdown speed.
  • DescentRate - Descent speed on the final approach (cm/s).
  • AgentRadiusCm - Radius of the landing body (cm), used to test the perch is big enough.
  • MaxSlopeDegrees - Steepest surface (degrees) the body may land on.
  • MaxDurationSeconds - Time limit (seconds): the node fails when it has not perched within this time. 0 = until aborted.
  • UseFlightMovementComponent - Drive the pawn through its LCM Flight Movement component when it has one; off moves the pawn directly.

Perceived Actor (service)

Settings (LcmBTService_PerceivedActor)

  • ResultKey - Receives the perceived actor (the closest, when several are).
  • RequiredTag - Only actors with this tag count (None = any perceived actor).
  • SightOnly - Only the SIGHT sense counts (off: any configured sense - hearing, damage...).
  • ForgetAfterSeconds - Keep the key this long after the last sighting, so the node using it can run its own search before the branch is abandoned (seconds).
  • LastKnownLocationKey - Optional (Vector): where the actor was last SEEN - kept after the actor key is forgotten, so an Investigate branch can search there. Cleared by whoever finishes the search.
  • LastKnownVelocityKey - Optional (Vector): the velocity it was last seen moving with (cm/s) - which way to search.
