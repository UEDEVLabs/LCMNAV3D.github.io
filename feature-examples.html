Feature Examples

Applies to LCM Nav3D 3.6.0 · Unreal Engine 5.2 to 5.8 · Updated 22 November 2026

Eight levels in the style of Epic's Content Examples: every display shows one feature of LCM Nav3D working, with a plaque that says what to look at and which chapter to read. They come with the free LCM Nav3D Samples download (LCM Nav3D menu → Get the Demo Levels (free)...), under Content/Showcase/; the demo menu lists them under Feature Examples.

The tables below are written out from the levels themselves, so they match what is in each level.

Using a level

  • Walk with W A S D and the mouse. A display starts when you walk up to it and resets when you leave.
  • Tab / Shift+Tab jump to the next / previous display. R replays the one you are at.
  • O switches the planned routes on and off (the Navigation Manager's Draw Flight Paths).
  • H hides the HUD; Esc returns to the demo menu.
  • Each display checks itself: its lines on the HUD turn PASS (green) or FAIL (red) as the demo runs.
  • Every tree, query and crowd config a display uses sits in Showcase/SC_0N/DN_M/, named for what it does; open it to see how the display is built.

The bodies the navigation flies: drone, jet, bird and fish, each with its own 6-DOF dynamics. They use the same nodes and the same paths, but each body has different physics. Tab jumps to the next display.

# Display What it shows Read
2.1 Four flight archetypes A drone, a jet, a bird and a fish fly the same kind of loop, each in its own lane. Flight Locomotion Reference, Archetypes

2.3 Tuning: agile and gentle Two drones on the same course. Agile: Max Tilt 60, Max Acceleration 2000. Gentle: 20 and 500. Flight Locomotion Reference, Archetypes
2.4 Jet: banked turns through gates A fixed-wing body cannot hover or turn in place: it banks, keeps flying speed and turns on a radius. Flight Locomotion Reference, Winged bodies

2.8 Hard turns: the speed governor A slalom of sharp turns at full speed. The follower plans its speed BACKWARDS from each corner: Flight Locomotion Reference, Speed planning
